About Me

I am a researcher and lecturer in social psychology and behavioral science at Charles University in Prague, based at the Faculty of Social Sciences (Institute of Communication Studies and Journalism) and the Peace Research Center Prague. I also collaborate on the ERC-funded MICROCODE project.

My research lies at the intersection of moral psychology, behavioral economics, and experimental methodology. I study how people make moral and (dis)honest decisions, how the public forms attitudes toward high-stakes security questions such as nuclear and autonomous weapons, and how psychological science can be made more replicable and credible.

I care deeply about open and reproducible science. Much of my work is preregistered, multi-lab, and cross-national — including large collaborations such as Many Labs, the Psychological Science Accelerator, and global tournaments on climate and health behavior published in Nature, Nature Human Behaviour, Science Advances, and PNAS.
