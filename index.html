About

I am a researcher and lecturer in social psychology and behavioral science at Charles University in Prague, based at the Institute of Communication Studies and Journalism and the Peace Research Center Prague. I also work on the ERC-funded MICROCODE project on the microfoundations of nuclear order.

My research sits at the intersection of moral psychology, behavioral economics, and experimental methodology. I study how people make moral and (dis)honest decisions, how the public forms attitudes toward high-stakes security questions such as nuclear and autonomous weapons, and how psychological science can be made more replicable.

A second strand of my work is metascience and open science. I have contributed to large collaborative projects — Many Labs, Registered Replication Reports, and the Psychological Science Accelerator — and to global tournaments on climate and health behaviour published in Nature, Nature Human Behaviour, Science Advances, and PNAS.

I run experimental work through the PLESS laboratory in Prague, with a participant pool of more than 5,000 volunteers. My ORCID record gives the fullest overview of my publications.
